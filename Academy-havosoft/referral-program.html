Client Referral Incentive Program Training

To encourage employee participation in Havosoft International Limited’s business development efforts by recognizing and rewarding successful client referrals, thereby supporting the company’s growth and outreach.

This policy applies to all full-time and part-time employees of Havosoft International Limited, except those whose roles already include commission-based incentives (e.g., Sales team members or others with client acquisition bonuses).

All employees are eligible to participate except:

  • Sales team members
  • Employees whose compensation includes client acquisition commissions or bonuses
  • The referred client must be new to Havosoft.
  • Employee must submit a Client Referral Form to Sales for approval.
  • Introduction can be made via email, memo, or in-person.
  • Referral must be logged and acknowledged before any engagement begins.

If a referred client signs an agreement and makes full payment:

  • Referring employee receives a one-time 5% commission of the net deal value.
  • Payment is made through payroll within 7–14 business days after full payment.
  • All payments are subject to tax and statutory deductions.
  • Incentives issued only after client agreement and full payment.
  • Management resolves multiple claims based on evidence and timing.
  • Employees must disclose any personal/financial connections.
  • Client consent required for data sharing (NDPR, GDPR).

All referral documentation and related communications will be handled with appropriate confidentiality. Information will only be shared with relevant internal parties for validation and processing, or as required by law.

Employees who submit referrals in good faith are protected from retaliation. No employee will face adverse consequences for participating in the referral program, even if a referral is not approved or does not result in a deal.

Disputes related to referral ownership or commission entitlement will be reviewed by a designated panel comprising members of HR, Sales, and Legal. The decision of Management is final and binding.
